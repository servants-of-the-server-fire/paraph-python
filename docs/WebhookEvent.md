# WebhookEvent

Event types. `request.created`, `request.success`, `request.cancelled`, `request.error`, and `signer.signed` are dispatched for their respective lifecycle events. `webhook.test` is sent when you use the test endpoint to verify your webhook URL. 

## Enum

* `REQUEST_DOT_CREATED` (value: `'request.created'`)

* `REQUEST_DOT_SUCCESS` (value: `'request.success'`)

* `REQUEST_DOT_CANCELLED` (value: `'request.cancelled'`)

* `REQUEST_DOT_ERROR` (value: `'request.error'`)

* `SIGNER_DOT_SIGNED` (value: `'signer.signed'`)

* `WEBHOOK_DOT_TEST` (value: `'webhook.test'`)
